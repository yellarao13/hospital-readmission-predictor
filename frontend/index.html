01 · THE PROJECT

What is our project?

HealthAI is a web application that estimates the chance of a patient being readmitted to the hospital within 30 days after they go home.

You enter details such as age, diagnosis, length of stay, number of medications and discharge type. The app learns from 1,000 real-style patient records and gives back a risk percentage and a clear Yes / No answer.

02 · THE PROBLEM

What does it solve?

Hospitals lose time, money and patient health when people come back soon after discharge. The hard part is knowing who will.

⚠️

Hidden high-risk patients

Staff cannot manually check every patient. The tool highlights who may need extra attention before they leave.

💸

Costly repeat admissions

Unplanned readmissions are expensive for hospitals and families. Early warning helps prevent them.

⏱️

Slow, manual decisions

Instead of guessing, a quick data-based estimate supports faster and more consistent follow-up planning.

    About HealthAI

    What this interface is, how it helps, and how it works.

    🏥 What is this interface?

    HealthAI is a hospital analytics web app that estimates the chance of a patient being readmitted to hospital within 30 days of discharge. You enter basic details about a patient's stay and the app returns a risk percentage with the main factors behind it.

    💡 How does it help?

    • Flag high-risk patients early so staff can plan follow-up calls, visits or extra discharge support.
    • Spot patterns in the Dashboard and Analytics pages — which diagnoses, admission types or discharge routes see more readmissions.
    • Keep a record of every prediction you make in Patient History, searchable and exportable to CSV.
    • Learn and demonstrate an end-to-end machine learning workflow in the browser.

    ⚙ How does it work?

    1. The app loads 1,000 patient records (embedded in this file, generated from the CSV).
    2. On start-up it trains a logistic regression model in your browser — numbers are standardised and categories one-hot encoded.
    3. When you press Predict, your inputs go through the same encoding and the model outputs a probability from 0–100%.
    4. The risk is shown as Low, Moderate or High, with the factors that pushed it up or down.
    5. The result is saved to your browser (localStorage) and appears in Patient History.

    🗂 Pages

    • Dashboard – totals, readmission rate, outcome split, age groups, diagnoses and recent records.
    • Predict – the patient form and risk result.
    • Patient History – your past predictions and the dataset records.
    • Analytics – Yes/No charts for every factor plus model quality.
    • About – this page.

    ⚠ Important note

    The model is trained on a small dataset and its accuracy is modest (see Analytics → Model Performance). Predictions are a learning demo, not medical advice, and must never replace a clinician's judgement.
