Our approach

Technology that supports human decisions

HealthAI is designed to organize model outputs in a straightforward way. Its goal is to help users review information, ask better questions, and keep context in view when interpreting a risk estimate.

Responsible use

Predictions can be uncertain and may reflect limitations in the data or model. Results should be interpreted alongside relevant clinical information and professional judgment.

  • Review the context behind each result.
  • Do not treat a prediction as a diagnosis.
  • Keep qualified professionals involved in decisions.
